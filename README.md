## 中文
此專案旨在以爬蟲方式記錄高雄市消防局對外公開之報案紀錄，其狀態以及派遣之分隊的追蹤，以便有需求之民眾進行調閱。

基於資料來源本身是公開的，惟因沒有紀錄的保存(例如狀態之變化，以及過往日期的資料)，故個人開發此簡易專案進行追蹤。

爬蟲每分鐘更新一次，本人理解資訊公開是為了便民，若個人此舉被認定為濫用、違法或侵權，再煩請進行告知處理，謝謝。 

為了讓有調閱需求之民眾或相關人員能夠回溯與查詢，個人獨立開發了此簡易專案，進行資料的統整與追蹤留存。


本專案僅作資訊的統整與歷史留存，**不保證資料的絕對準確性與即時性**，實際災情與派遣情況請以官方公告為準。

## English

This project aims to collect and archive publicly available incident reports released by the Kaohsiung City Fire Department, tracking their status and the dispatched fire brigade units so that the historical record and information can be looked up and reviewed by public who is in need.

Although the data is publicly available, the original source does not retain historical records, such as changes in incident status or reports from previous dates. To address this limitation, I independently developed this simple project to track, organize, and preserve the data over time.


The scraper updates the data once every minute. I understand that public information is intended to serve the public interest. If this project is deemed to constitute misuse, a violation of applicable laws, or an infringement of rights, please feel free to contact me so that I can address the matter accordingly. Thank you.

This project is independently developed for the sole purpose of organizing publicly available information and maintaining historical records, allowing members of the public and relevant personnel to retrieve and review past information when needed.


**This project does not guarantee the absolute accuracy or real-time availability of the data.** For actual emergencies, incident details, and dispatch information, please refer to official announcements and information released by the relevant authorities.
